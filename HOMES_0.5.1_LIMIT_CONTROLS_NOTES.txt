Chill Zone Homes 0.5.1-alpha-home-limit-controls
Target: Minecraft 26.3 / Fabric

HOME PROGRESSION
- Home 1 is free.
- Home 2 costs 100 Shards.
- Home 3 costs 150 Shards.
- Home 4 costs 250 Shards.
- Every additional slot after Home 3 costs +100 Shards.
- Home 28 costs 2,650 Shards.
- Existing /home, /homes GUI, home-name autocomplete, /pay, /bal, /baltop and Shard systems are retained.

ADMIN HOME LIMIT CONTROLS
- /homes limit <player> <1-28>
  Sets a hard staff cap. It does not delete saved homes or purchased progression.

- /homes limit <player> reset
  Resets the player's purchased slot progression to 1, but DOES NOT remove the current hard staff cap.
  Example: set hard limit to 1, then reset -> player remains capped at 1 and cannot buy more.
  Saved home locations are preserved rather than deleted.

- /homes limit <player> remove
  Removes the hard staff cap. The player's normal Shard-purchased allowance applies again.
  If reset was used first, the player resumes at 1 and can buy slots again normally.

- /homes limit <player> undo
  Restores the purchased slot allowance saved by the most recent reset and removes the hard staff cap.
  This is the recovery option if you want to give the player's previous slot allowance back.

EXISTING INDIVIDUAL HOME REMOVAL
- /homes remove <player> <home>
  Still deletes one actual saved home.
  Permission: chillzonehomes.command.removehome

OWNER PERMISSION TO MAKE /homes remove APPEAR IN AUTOCOMPLETE
Shockbyte console:
  lp group owner permission set chillzonehomes.command.removehome true

LIMIT ADMIN PERMISSION
  chillzonehomes.command.limit
