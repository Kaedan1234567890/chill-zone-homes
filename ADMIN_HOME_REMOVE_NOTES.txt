Chill Zone Homes 0.4.1-alpha-admin-home-remove

Added:
- /homes remove <player> <home>
- Player autocomplete includes players previously observed by Chill Zone Homes, including offline players.
- Home autocomplete shows the selected player's saved homes.
- Removing a home deletes it immediately from chill_zone_homes.json and therefore from that player's Homes UI.
- New LuckPerms permission: chillzonehomes.command.removehome
- Console is allowed automatically.

Grant example:
/lp group owner permission set chillzonehomes.command.removehome true

Existing /homes limit permission remains chillzonehomes.command.limit.
